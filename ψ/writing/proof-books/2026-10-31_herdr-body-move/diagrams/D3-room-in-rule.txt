        members you typed          w7:p3 (new)  w7:p2 (wt)
                 │
                 ▼
        is the room's space open? ──no──► members as typed
                 │ yes
                 ▼
        inside = every pane in that space, right now
                 │              w7:p3  w7:p2  w7:p1 (old, still open)
                 ▼
        first typed member inside? ──yes──► typed + (inside not typed)
                 │ no                        w7:p3 w7:p2 + w7:p1
                 ▼
        inside + (typed not inside)
                 │
                 ▼
        3 members ──► 3 columns ──► state saved with all 3

   state file  = order only. Editing it does not remove a pane.
   way out     = the pane leaves the space:  pane close  or  herdr-room out
