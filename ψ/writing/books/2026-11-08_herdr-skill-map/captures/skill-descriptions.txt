## herdr-dissolve  (first date in folder: 2026-08-22)
The reverse of /herdr-wt, and the one skill for winding herdr down — from a single worktree up to the whole machine. A worktree whose agent did work gets saved first (its own agent commits, pushes, opens a signed PR and reports to its PARENT, the repo's main-checkout agent); a worktree with nothing in it, a merged or pushed one, or a space on a missing folder is removed straight away through herdr so the sidebar and git agree; idle agents on the main checkout are quit and their panes closed; checkouts that are only behind are fast-forwarded.
