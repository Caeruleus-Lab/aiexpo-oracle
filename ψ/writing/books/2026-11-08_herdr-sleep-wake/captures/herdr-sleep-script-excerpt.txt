#   herdr-sleep sleep <pane>=<label> ...   [--session nsm] [--no-bookmark] [--keep-space]

#   herdr-sleep list  [--day YYYY-MM-DD]
#

#   ~/.local/state/herdr-sleep/<day>/<label>.json (cwd, session id, argv, --channels, git state, bookmark);

#   `cd <cwd> && direnv exec . <argv with --resume <session>>`, renames the agent, briefs it with its bookmark,
#   and stamps "woke" on the card. Cards are never deleted: they are the sleep log.
# Never writes env values or tokens into a card. Never commits, pushes or deletes files.
