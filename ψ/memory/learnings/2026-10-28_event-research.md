# AI Expo Thailand 2026: public research (28 Oct)

## Event
- 5–8 Nov 2026, 10:00–20:00 daily, Challenger Hall 1, IMPACT Muang Thong Thani (17,136 m²).
- Co-located with Thailand Mobile Expo in Hall 2. The two halls connect.
- Organizer: M Vision PCL (บมจ.เอ็ม วิชั่น). Contact: info@aiexpothailand.com.
- Scale: 70+ speakers, 51 sessions, 2 stages (Business = users, Builder = builders), 100+ booths.
- Travel: MRT Pink Line to Muang Thong Thani branch, IMPACT (MT01), Exit 3 skywalk. Parking P1, ~2,200 cars.

## Tickets: who will be in the room
- Free entry (Luma QR) covers the Public Area and Free Zone only. **It does not include the Masterclass Zone.**
- Masterclass Regular 4-Day Pass: 2,000 baht. 1-Day and VIP passes also exist (prices not listed).
- So people at the workshop have paid for a pass. Expect builders who chose to come, not walk-ins.
- Attendees get e-Certificates (up to 10). Replays are available for 1 month. **The session will probably be recorded.**

## What this means for prep
- **Zero changeover.** Vibe Coding 101 ends at 15:05, the same minute we start. Laptop, HDMI and wifi checks have to happen before 14:00 or during the break.
- **A 50-minute gap after us.** That leaves room for Q&A or a hands-on help desk after 16:10. Ask พี่เอ๋อ whether the stage stays free.
- **Two agent workshops come earlier the same day** (10:30 and 11:35). The room may already have heard "AI agent" twice. Our edge is memory that survives a restart, not agents as such.
- **A Claude Code session follows at 17:00.** Some people may stay for both. We can point to it as a next step.
- **Name spelling differs.** The agenda prints "เทพปรียะพล". The repo and poster notes use "เทพปริยะพล". Check with คุณยิ้ม which one is correct.
